Our Story

A Cherished Independent Gem by the Harbour

Tucked along Regent Quay with the working harbour just outside the window, Characters Coffee House has spent decades pouring proper coffee and dishing out honest, comforting food to the folk who make this corner of Aberdeen tick.

From port workers grabbing a quick flat white before their shift to regulars settling in with the paper and a pot of tea, we've built our name on warm hospitality, familiar faces, and food that tastes like it was made by someone who cares — because it is.

Every soup is homemade, every scone baked fresh, and every cup pulled with the same care we've always put into it. Come as a stranger, leave as a regular.

  • Family-run, independent, and proudly local
  • Homemade soups & bakes, made fresh every day
  • A true harbour-side home for coffee lovers
